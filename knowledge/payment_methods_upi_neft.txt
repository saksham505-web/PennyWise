TITLE: Digital payment methods: UPI, NEFT, RTGS, IMPS and more (sources: RBI FAME 2024, NCFE handbook 2024)
NEFT, RTGS and IMPS are the main bank transfer channels. NEFT and RTGS run 24x7, even on holidays, per the 2024 RBI and NCFE booklets. RTGS is for large payments, with a minimum of Rs. 2 lakh and no maximum. IMPS is instant with a maximum of Rs. 5 lakh. NEFT has no minimum or maximum. Limits can change, so check with your bank.

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) transfers money between bank accounts using a smartphone. You need a bank account, a mobile number linked to it and internet. You use BHIM or any bank or third-party UPI app, link your account and create a UPI PIN (a debit card is needed once for setup). You can pay using a virtual payment address (UPI ID), a mobile number or a QR code, so you do not share account details. Transfers work instantly, 24x7. UPI can also link to RuPay credit cards. For feature phones, UPI123Pay offers options such as missed call and IVR.

Other methods: *99# (USSD) allows basic mobile banking without internet. Mobile wallets hold money loaded from your account. Prepaid cards spend up to the amount loaded. Debit and credit cards use a PIN and OTP for security. Micro ATMs and AEPS (Aadhaar-enabled payments) are used through bank business correspondents.

Advantages of digital payments: fast, convenient, low cost and a digital record you can track.

UPI Dos: keep the app updated, review collect requests before accepting, and recheck the amount and the name before paying. Don'ts: never share your PIN, and never pay without verifying the recipient. See the UPI safety lesson for scams.

TITLE: Government schemes: Jan Dhan, PMSBY, PMJJBY and Ayushman Bharat (source: NCFE handbook 2024)
These details are from the NCFE handbook of September 2024. Premiums, limits and eligibility can change, so confirm on the official websites.

Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) is a financial inclusion mission. It opens a basic savings account for people without one, with no minimum balance, interest on deposits and a RuPay debit card. Accident cover comes with the card: Rs. 2 lakh for accounts opened after 28 August 2018 (Rs. 1 lakh earlier). Eligible holders can get an overdraft of up to Rs. 10,000. The accounts also receive direct benefit transfers and can link to the insurance and pension schemes below.

Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a one-year renewable accident insurance. Account holders aged 18 to 70 can join through auto-debit. It pays Rs. 2 lakh on accidental death or total disability, for a premium of Rs. 20 a year.

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) is a one-year renewable life insurance for account holders aged 18 to 50 with auto-debit. It pays Rs. 2 lakh on death due to any cause, for a premium of Rs. 436 a year.

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is a government health assurance scheme giving a cover of Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for hospitalisation in empanelled public and private hospitals. The cover is shared by the whole family, it is cashless, it covers pre-existing conditions from day one, and it is portable across India. It is meant for eligible families, so check eligibility at pmjay.gov.in.

Atal Pension Yojana is covered in the retirement lesson. More details: jansuraksha.gov.in and pmjdy.gov.in.

TITLE: Credit score and credit report (sources: RBI FAME 2024, NCFE handbook 2024)
A credit score is a three-digit number showing how reliably you repay loans. It usually ranges from 300 to 900, and higher is better. It comes along with your credit information report from a credit information company. India has four credit bureaus: CIBIL, Experian, CRIF High Mark and Equifax. Lenders check your score when you apply for a loan or card. A higher score generally means easier approval and a lower interest rate, and a low score may get an application rejected.

How to build a good score:
1. Pay EMIs and card bills on time. This is the most important factor.
2. Borrow only within your means and never miss an instalment.
3. Do not apply for many loans at once. Many enquiries look like credit-hungry behaviour and can lower your score.
4. Repaying a loan early can help build a good credit history.
5. Review your score periodically, for example every six months.

Free report: under RBI rules you can get one free full credit report, including the score, once every calendar year from each credit information company. The steps are on each company's website.

Errors: if your report has a mistake, ask the credit company or the lender to correct it. If the complaint remains unresolved for more than 30 days, or you are not satisfied, you can complain to the RBI Ombudsman through cms.rbi.org.in.

Students often have no score because they have no credit history. It builds slowly through responsible borrowing, such as a small loan or card repaid on time. There is no substitute for discipline in paying loans on time.

TITLE: Digital lending apps and safe borrowing (sources: RBI FAME 2024, NCFE handbook 2024)
Digital lending apps (DLAs) let you apply for a loan on your phone. Legitimate ones belong to banks and NBFCs, or to lending service providers working for them. Many fake or abusive apps also exist.

RBI's advice on responsible borrowing:
1. Borrow to buy assets that create value or returns, for example a home loan or an education loan. Do not use credit irresponsibly to fund spending.
2. Borrow from regulated entities (banks, NBFCs, housing finance companies). They are supervised, more transparent, do not overcharge like moneylenders, and give a free complaint system.
3. Beware of agents who ask a fee to help you get a loan. Deal directly with the bank, NBFC or business correspondent.
4. Be careful about offers cheaper than banks. They may collect a processing fee and disappear.

Before using a lending app:
1. Check on the bank's or NBFC's own website that the app is linked to them.
2. Avoid apps sent as links by SMS or social media.
3. Check the total cost of the loan, the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) and the Key Fact Statement (KFS).
4. Read the sanction letter and privacy policy. Documents should be digitally signed and on the bank's or NBFC's letterhead.
5. Check the permissions the app asks for, and protect your data. Be suspicious if it wants access to contacts or photos.
6. Report suspicious apps to law enforcement (call 1930 or use cybercrime.gov.in).

Complaints: contact the grievance redressal officer listed on the app or lender's website. If not resolved within 30 days, complain on the RBI CMS portal (cms.rbi.org.in).
